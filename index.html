How the maths works

battery size = (daily load to cover × days of cover) ÷ (depth of discharge × efficiency)

  • Daily load to cover — your night-time energy for self-consumption, or your full daily use for backup.
  • Days of cover — 1 for daily cycling, or your chosen days of autonomy in an outage.
  • Depth of discharge — batteries shouldn't be drained to zero: lithium 90%, lead-acid 50%.
  • Efficiency — round-trip losses from charging, storing and inverting ≈ 85%.

The raw capacity is what you'd buy; the usable energy is what you actually get out (raw × DoD × efficiency). Peak sun hours are Australia-specific summer/winter averages by capital city; winter is the sizing season in the south.

This is an estimate for planning, not a professional design. Confirm sizing with a CEC-accredited installer who accounts for your real load profile, roof orientation, shading and local network rules.
